SPADEX-1: Earth Viewing Data for MMX
SPADEX (Space-craft Docking Experiment) is based on the low docking concept using two small satellites. Docking refers to engaging and securing the two spacecraft together by a docking mechanism when the approaching spacecraft called the “SDX01” flies directly into another spacecraft called the “SDX02” in a controlled manner.
The primary objectives of SPADEX experiment are to:
To Develop and demonstrate the technology needed for rendezvous and docking, using two small satellites.
The secondary mission objectives are to:
Demonstrate the controllability of SDX01 & SDX02 in the docked condition.

Processing Steps
Data decoding by extraction of raw image bands (Blue, Red and Green) from payload data files.
Radiometric correction for all bands using Look Up Table(LUT) application to convert raw image bands to radio-metrically corrected bands.
Geometric Correction for removing geometric distortions in image and generating geo corrected products in WGS-84 datum.
Band-to-Band registration for producing geo corrected BBR product in Natural Color Composite(NCC) format i.e. RGB bands
.
References
[1]
Lowe, David G. "Distinctive image features from scale-invariant keypoints."
International journal of computer vision
60.2 (2004): 91-110.
Derivation Techniques and Algorithm
Band-to-Band registration employs Scale Invariant Feature Transform (SIFT) for detecting keypoints across separate bands that are used for image registration. SIFT has been described in detail in [1].

Known problems with data
Since bands have temporal shift and clouds are moving features, clouds may appear slightly mis-registered.

,Ex:MMXXXMU2P1SD20407001NNNN25264054328469_V1_1_BBR_geo_final.tif where:
'PLD'

'STN'
stands for Station-id, ex: MU2.
'P#'

'SAT'

'ORBNO'
corresponds to the orbit number, ex: 04070
'
YYDDDHHMMSSMSE
'

is Year Integer Value.
‘
